THE FISH'S RULES (try turning one off)

What's going on Each fish only sees its nearest neighbours and follows four rules: don't crowd them, swim the way they swim, stay close to them, and flee the shark; if a neighbour panics, panic too.
Nobody leads. The swirling ball, the splits and merges, the tornado shapes and the flash of panic rippling through the school all emerge.
