LOOP LOGIC

Drag any loop to move it. Change the speed and watch the track re-sculpt itself.

The rules Each train: gravity pulls it along the slope · friction slows it · it brakes if it gets too close to the train ahead. The station launches it.
Each hill & loop: grows a little when a train makes it over, shrinks when a train fails.
Nobody designs the final ride. It shapes itself to the edge of what the launch speed can handle.
